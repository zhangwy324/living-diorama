Proof-of-concept build for Windows 11: draw a creature and release it into the diorama.

The installer is **not code-signed**, so Windows SmartScreen will warn that it comes from an unknown publisher. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for the current user only and needs no admin rights. You can install it over 0.1.0; your settings are kept.

## What's new

- **Draw a creature.** Open the **Creation Studio** with the pencil button on the strip or from the tray menu. Draw your creature facing right with its feet on the guide line, give it a name and a size, and **Release** it. It walks into the world as a solid 3D body built from your drawing. A turning 3D preview beside the canvas shows the body as you draw, and hints warn about parts that may not work well, such as very thin parts or a size too big to cross the wooden bridge.
- **My creatures.** Every creature you release is saved and survives a relaunch. The panel beside the preview lists them: **+** adds another to the world, **−** takes the newest one out, and **Delete** removes a creature for good after asking. The world holds up to 20 creatures.
- **Solid bodies.** Creatures are no longer flat cards that turn towards the camera: drawn creatures and the mossling are solid bodies extruded from their drawings, and they cast shadows from the sun. Their sides take their colours from the edge of the drawing, so thin limbs and separate shapes keep their own colours and an outline doesn't paint the sides. The mossling has its hairs back.
- **A new expanded mode.** **Expand** now turns the strip into an ordinary window you can move, resize, maximize and Alt+Tab to, and gives the strip's space at the bottom of the screen back while it is open. The first time it opens maximized; after that it remembers its size and place. **F11** or the **Full screen** button toggles full screen, **Esc** leaves full screen and **Home** resets the camera. **Collapse**, the window's close button or the tray turns it back into the strip.

## Known limitations

- The world starts fresh on every launch, with the three mosslings and none of your creatures. Add them back from My creatures.
- A drawing you haven't released is not saved. Closing the Studio asks first, but quitting the app doesn't.
- Every body is as thick as 40% of its height, and holes in a drawing are filled in. Very thin parts can look flat.
- Large creatures are too big to cross the wooden bridge and stay on their side of the river.
- Strokes have jagged edges, worst with the thickest brush, and there is no fill tool yet.
- Windows 11 only.
